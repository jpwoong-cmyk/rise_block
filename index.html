PUBLIC DATA SOURCES

What the tracker is built from

V1.4.1 separates source-backed facts from schematic geometry and stores community observations in Supabase. Unit stack/type and residential-level data is reconciled against the public unit-distribution charts. 3D coordinates, paths and simplified building masses are orientation sketches, not HDB BIM/survey data. Live unit status comes only from community reports; unreported units remain untracked.
